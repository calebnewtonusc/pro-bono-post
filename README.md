# The Pro Bono Post

An unofficial, deeply affectionate fan paper about consulting club life, in the
general shape of The Onion.

**Live:** https://calebnewtonusc.github.io/pro-bono-post/

## What this is not

It is not affiliated with 180 Degrees Consulting, its USC branch, its global
network, or any of its clients. Nobody in 180 approved it, edited it, or knew
what it would say. Every headline, quote, and statistic is invented.

## The one editorial rule

**Punch at the experience, never at the people.** The jokes are about slide
counts, week-eight silence, saying "capacity" too much, and the specific
indignity of rewriting a two hundred word essay fourteen times. They are never
about a real member, a real client, or a real nonprofit, and they never put words
in a real person's mouth.

If a joke would embarrass an actual human being, it does not go in.

## Submit a headline

Open a pull request against `index.html`. A good one is:

- **True first, funny second.** The best entries here are just accurate.
- **Affectionate.** Written by somebody who obviously likes the thing.
- **Specific.** "Consultants are nerds" is not a joke. "Team spends forty
  minutes on a font, delivers measurable community impact anyway" is.
- **Under twenty words** for a headline, one sentence for the subhead.

## If something lands wrong

Open an issue. It comes down the same day, no argument.
