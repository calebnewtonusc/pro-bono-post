# Join the masthead

The Post is staffed entirely by volunteers who put themselves here on purpose.

**If you are in 180 and you want your name in this paper, add it yourself.**
That is the only way anybody gets in. Nobody gets scraped, nobody gets
surprised, and if you ever want out you delete your own line and it is gone.

## How

1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in the GitHub web editor (pencil icon).
2. Find the `.staff` paragraph near the bottom.
3. Add yourself with a beat nobody would actually want:

```
Your Name, Senior Correspondent for Slide 41 &middot;
```

4. Commit, open a pull request, done.

Want a photo next to it? Add the file to `staff/` and link it in your PR. Your
photo, your call, your pull request. That is the whole consent model and it is
non-negotiable.

## How to submit a headline instead

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Headlines are about roles and
situations, never about a named person. That rule does not change.

## Getting out

Delete your line, open a PR, or just message Caleb. No questions, same day.
